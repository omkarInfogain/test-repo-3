# Step 02 — Draft the story narrative and scope

**Goal:** state what the story delivers and where its boundaries are.

## Actions

1. Write the story statement: "As a &lt;role&gt;, I want &lt;capability&gt;, so that &lt;benefit&gt;."
2. State what is explicitly IN scope and OUT of scope for this story.

## Output

Populate `story_statement`, `in_scope`, and `out_of_scope` in the template.
